LeetArxiv is a Substack publication by Murage Kibicho that bridges the gap between academic research and practical coding. It focuses on implementing papers from Arxiv—a repository of scientific preprints—into working code using the reader's preferred programming language. The approach mirrors Leetcode-style problem solving, making complex research more accessible and hands-on.

The publication targets developers, data scientists, and machine learning engineers who want to understand cutting-edge research by building it. By translating theoretical Arxiv papers into executable implementations, LeetArxiv helps subscribers deepen their technical skills and stay current with advancements in AI and computer science. It serves as both an educational tool and a practical resource for real-world application.

LeetArxiv has attracted over 1,000 subscribers since its launch. "Leetcode for implementing Arxiv papers in your preferred language." This concise tagline captures the core mission and resonates with a tech-savvy audience looking to merge research literacy with coding proficiency.
