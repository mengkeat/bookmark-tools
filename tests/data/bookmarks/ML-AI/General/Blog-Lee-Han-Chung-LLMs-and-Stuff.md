Han Lee's blog chronicles experiences and insights in building machine learning systems across enterprise environments and startups. Topics include LLM agent environments, prompt deployment failures, and the evolution of AI reasoning systems.

Key themes include the application of statistical rigor to AI/ML systems with a four-part series on statistics covering pass@k, confidence intervals, and agreement metrics between agents. Lee explores enterprise AI transformation through a "4-Set Framework for IT 3.0" and argues that "Agents Are Workflows."

He critiques overreliance on tech influencers and advocates for first-principles thinking in evaluating LLMs. On development practices, he highlights Pydantic for avoiding "spaghetti code" while warning of an impending "AI Winter Is Coming" amid inflated expectations.
