Loss landscape analysis examines the geometry of the optimization surface in neural networks. Key questions include how the shape of the loss landscape affects generalization, the relationship between flat minima and good generalization, and how different optimizers navigate the landscape.

Research has shown that neural networks have highly non-convex loss surfaces with many saddle points and local minima. The "flat vs sharp minima" debate relates to whether models converging to flat regions generalize better than those in sharp valleys.
