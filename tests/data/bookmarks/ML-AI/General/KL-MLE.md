KL divergence (Kullback-Leibler divergence) measures how one probability distribution differs from another. It plays a central role in maximum likelihood estimation and variational inference.

The relationship between KL divergence and MLE is fundamental: minimizing KL divergence between the true data distribution and model distribution is equivalent to maximizing the likelihood of the data under the model. This connection underpins many machine learning algorithms including variational autoencoders (VAEs) and generative models.
