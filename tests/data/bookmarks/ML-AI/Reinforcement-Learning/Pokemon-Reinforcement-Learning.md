A research team developed an RL agent capable of beating Pokémon Red using a policy with fewer than 10 million parameters—60,500 times smaller than models like DeepSeekV3. The breakthrough is a novel technique for generating solutions to complex, long-horizon tasks.

Pokémon Red presents uniquely difficult challenges: long gameplay (25 hours average), complex decision-making, multi-task objectives, and opaque reward structures. Unlike simpler Atari benchmarks, it requires strategic planning across battles, exploration, and resource management.

"With RL, we built an agent with a super tiny neural network with no pretraining... and we still achieved amazing results." Key tools included PyBoy (Python Game Boy emulator) and work by the Pokémon Reverse Engineering Team (PRET) enabling deep game state introspection.
