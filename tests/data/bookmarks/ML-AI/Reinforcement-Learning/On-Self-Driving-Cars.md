This repository hosts open course materials from a Coursera Specialization by University of Toronto on autonomous vehicle technology. It includes lecture videos, subtitles, PDFs, and notebooks covering four main courses: Introduction to Self-Driving Cars, State Estimation and Localization, Visual Perception, and Motion Planning.

Students engage with real-world data and use the CARLA simulator to implement algorithms in a realistic 3D environment. Prerequisites include linear algebra, probability, calculus, physics, control theory, and Python programming.

Market projections suggest a $42-billion self-driving car market and over 20 million autonomous vehicles by 2025. "hope everyone can enjoy it !!!" The repository is mirrored on Coding.net for improved accessibility in China.
